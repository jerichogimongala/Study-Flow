# Search visibility

StudyFlow includes a descriptive page title and description, a canonical URL, social sharing metadata, WebSite and WebApplication structured data, a crawler policy in `robots.txt`, and a single-page XML sitemap. The guide and questions are included directly in the HTML so search engines can read them without running the planner.

After deployment, verify ownership of `https://studyflow-exam-planner.netlify.app/` in Google Search Console and submit `https://studyflow-exam-planner.netlify.app/sitemap.xml`. Inspect the homepage URL and request indexing. Bing Webmaster Tools also accepts this sitemap.

Indexing and rankings depend on search engines and are not guaranteed. Monitor search impressions, queries and indexing reports over time. Relevant links from education resources and useful, original study content can support discovery.

If the site moves to a custom domain, update the canonical URL, Open Graph URL, structured data URLs, robots.txt sitemap reference and sitemap URL together. Redirect the previous domain to the new one.

Keep descriptions and structured data accurate as features change. Do not add invented reviews or ratings. The page does not claim FAQ rich results, which Google generally reserves for eligible government and health sites.
